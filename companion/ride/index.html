Target speed

Any steady speed calibrates equally well, so pick one that's comfortable on your street; the posted limit is a good choice. The phone reads what you actually drove, so you needn't hit it exactly.

How to calibrate with it

  1. Start VST tracking at home, then start recording here before you set off. Leave this screen open; a passenger or a phone mount is safest.
  2. Drive past the camera at a steady speed, near your target. Once each way on a two-way street.
  3. Stop recording and save the file to the computer running VST Studio (AirDrop, email, a USB cable or a shared folder).
  4. On VST Studio's Calibrate page, pick your pass and press Read my speed from a dashcam clip or ride-along.

Phone GPS speed is good to about half a mph at a steady speed. Your recording stays on this phone until you save or share it; this page sends nothing anywhere.
